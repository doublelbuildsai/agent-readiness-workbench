# Summit Gear Co. — Payment Terms Policy

## Standard Terms

| Term | Eligibility |
|------|-------------|
| Net-30 | Default for all accounts |
| Net-45 | Accounts with 2+ years history, no overdue invoices |
| Net-60 | Tier-1 retail only; requires Finance approval for deals >$250K |

## Non-Standard Terms

- Net-60 on deals above $250K: Finance approval required.
- Net-90: CFO approval only; strategic accounts with board-level contract.
- Prepayment discounts (2% for net-10): auto-approved for any deal size.

## Risk Factors

Payment term extensions combined with discounts above 15% increase deal risk score by 0.15.
