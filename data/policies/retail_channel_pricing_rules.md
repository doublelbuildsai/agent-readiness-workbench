# Summit Gear Co. — Retail Channel Pricing Rules

## Partner Tiers

| Tier | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Tier-1 Retail | National chains, 500+ stores | Northline Retail Group |
| Tier-2 Retail | Regional chains, 50–499 stores | Cascade Outfitters |
| Specialty | Independent / co-op buyers | Peak & Pine Co-op |

## Tier-1 Retail Caps

- **Maximum discount without VP Sales approval: 15%**
- Deals above 15% require VP Sales + Finance parallel approval regardless of rep authority.
- Tier-1 accounts have existing volume rebate agreements — see account record before applying promotional pricing.

## Competitive Response

- Competitive match requests require documentation of competitor offer.
- Emergency competitive responses above 18% require CRO notification within 24 hours.

## Floor-Set Deadlines

- Q4 floor-set deals closing within 7 days receive priority routing but do NOT bypass discount caps.
