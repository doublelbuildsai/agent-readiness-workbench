# Summit Gear Co. — Discount Authority Matrix

Effective: 2026-01-01

## Standard Discount Thresholds

| Role | Max Discount (without escalation) | Max Deal Size |
|------|-----------------------------------|---------------|
| Sales Rep | 10% | $150,000 |
| Regional Director | 15% | $350,000 |
| VP Sales | 20% | $1,000,000 |
| CFO / Finance | Required for any deal >20% or >$500K with non-standard terms |

## Rules

1. Discounts above a rep's authority require escalation to the next tier.
2. Any discount above 20% requires VP Sales AND Finance parallel approval.
3. Tier-1 retail partners have additional caps (see retail_channel_pricing_rules.md).
4. Promotional discounts follow separate rules (see promotional_pricing_policy.md).

## Audit Requirement

All approved discounts must be logged with: approver identity, policy citation, timestamp, and final discount percentage.
