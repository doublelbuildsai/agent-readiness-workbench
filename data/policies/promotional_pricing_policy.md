# Summit Gear Co. — Promotional Pricing Policy

## Promotional Discounts

- Promotional discounts are time-bound (typically 30–90 days).
- Promotional periods: Q1 Spring Launch, Q3 Back-to-School, Q4 Holiday Floor-Set.

## Stacking Rules (CRITICAL)

- **Promotional discounts CANNOT stack with volume rebates.**
- If an account has an active volume rebate (e.g., 5% annual rebate), promotional discount replaces — does not add to — the rebate for that order period.
- Violation of stacking rules requires Finance review and may void the quote.

## Approval

- Promo discounts 10–15%: Regional Director
- Promo discounts 16–20%: VP Sales
- Promo discounts above 20%: VP Sales + Finance (parallel)

## Documentation

Every promotional quote must cite: promo window, eligible SKUs, and confirmation that volume rebates are excluded for the promo period.
